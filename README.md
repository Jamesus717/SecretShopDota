# 🏪 SecretShop Dota

> *Your favourite item shop, now a community.*

**SecretShop** is a Dota 2 community and Discord server built by players, for players. Whether you're a 1k Herald who keeps buying Boots of Travel on minute 8, or an Ancient trying to finally crack Divine — there's a spot here for you. We run in-house leagues, organised matchmaking, and a space where the only flame allowed is the kind that makes you a better player.

---

## 🧭 What We're About

We started SecretShop because we wanted a place where Dota players could actually **play Dota** — not just sit in queue. Real games. Real teammates. Real improvement.

- 🎮 **In-House Games** — Organised lobbies where you know who you're playing with
- 🏆 **Community Leagues** — Structured competition across skill brackets so every rank has something to fight for
- 📈 **Get Better** — We're building toward game overviews, personal stats, and post-game breakdowns so you can actually learn from your games
- 🤝 **Good Vibes** — A community that takes the game seriously without taking itself too seriously

## 🎮 SecretLeague

SecretLeague is our flagship in-house tournament format. Players can sign up solo or as a full 5-stack. We handle the seeding, scheduling, and scorekeeping — you just show up and play.

**How to enter:**
1. Join the Discord (link below)
2. Fill out the registration form pinned in `#sign-ups`
3. Get assigned to your bracket
4. Win (or lose gracefully and learn something)

## 📖 Community Guidelines

We keep it simple:

1. **Don't be a dick.** Flame the draft, not the person.
2. **Show up.** If you sign up for a game, be there. Flakers get sat out next time.
3. **All ranks welcome.** We have brackets for everyone. Skill-shaming is an instant mute.
4. **English in main channels**, other languages welcome in regional channels.
5. **Have fun.** It's a video game. Cry after the game, not during it.
